Cape Leeuwin

Cape Leeuwin is the south-western corner of the "squarish sticky out bit". Close by is the town of Augusta. The rocky coast and white sandy beaches are spectacular.

Driving south from Margaret River we pass though some magnificant Karri forests along the way to our next stop at Augusta.

The Cape Leeuwin lighthouse sits on the very most south-western tip of Australia. This is where early sailors often bumped into the continent that would go onto to be known as Australia...

The pristine natural enviroment is the magnet that attracts visitors to the region from across the globe. The rocky coastline, turquiose waters, ocean swells, and white sandy beaches area photographer's dream...

The photographer also found plenty of wildflowers to photograph too...

Despite appearences the "BEST FISH & CHIPS" in Australia can be found in Augusta.
