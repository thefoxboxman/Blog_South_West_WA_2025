Esperance

Esperance. The last civilizaton on the coast for over 1200km until Ceduna in South Australia. Esperence and Ceduna are at opposite ends of the Great Australian Bight. Esperence is a natural deep water harbour protected by an archpelago of offshore islands. The town while small is exudes character and belief in itself.
